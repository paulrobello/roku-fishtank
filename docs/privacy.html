Privacy policy

Reef Tank for Roku · Effective November 14, 2026

This policy describes the Reef Tank Roku screensaver maintained by Paul Robello and this project website.

The Roku screensaver

Reef Tank runs locally on your Roku. The current app does not transmit personal information to its developer, operate a developer server, require an account, or include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not sell or share personal information.

Settings stored on your device

The app stores your fish-density, camera-drift, and night-mode preferences in the Roku device registry. These preferences are used locally to customize the screensaver and are not sent to the developer. You can change them in the screensaver settings.

Day and night lighting

Automatic lighting uses the device's date, local time, and time-zone offset with a fixed reference location of Woodland, California included in the app. It does not request, detect, or send your location.

Roku and this website

Roku operates the device, account, and distribution platform under its own privacy policy. This policy does not describe or control Roku's independent data practices.

This website uses no added analytics, advertising scripts, or forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes and handles site visits and GitHub interactions under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support and children

If you choose to post a support request on GitHub, your post and any details you include may be public. Do not post passwords or other private information. The screensaver is intended for a general audience and does not collect personal information from children.

Questions and changes

For questions, use the support page. If app behavior changes, this policy will be updated here with a revised effective date.
